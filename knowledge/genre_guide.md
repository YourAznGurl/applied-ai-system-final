# Genre Guide

## ambient
Ambient music is slow, spacious and atmospheric with little rhythm. It suits sleep, meditation and quiet moments.

## classical
Classical music is acoustic and orchestral, often calm and detailed. It suits quiet focus and peaceful moments.

## country
Country music is story-driven with warm guitars and a nostalgic feel. It suits road memories and relaxed evenings.

## edm
EDM is electronic dance music with big drops and fast, steady beats. It is high energy and suits parties and festivals.

## folk
Folk music is acoustic and personal, often quiet and emotional. It suits reflective or sad moods.

## hip hop
Hip hop is rhythm-driven with strong beats and confident delivery. It suits motivation and walking with swagger.

## indie pop
Indie pop is light, friendly and a little quirky. It suits mornings, walks and gentle happy moods.

## jazz
Jazz is warm and relaxed with improvised melodies. It suits coffee shops, dinners and calm evenings.

## lofi
Lofi is mellow, low-tempo and often instrumental with a warm, slightly fuzzy sound. It is popular for studying and relaxing.

## metal
Metal is loud and heavy with fast drums and distorted guitars. It is very high energy and suits aggressive moods.

## pop
Pop is bright, catchy and built around memorable hooks. It has medium-to-high energy and suits parties, drives and happy moods.

## r&b
R&B is smooth and soulful with warm vocals. It suits romantic and late-evening moods.

## reggae
Reggae has a relaxed, off-beat rhythm and a sunny feel. It suits beaches and easygoing afternoons.

## rock
Rock uses guitars and strong drums. It tends to be loud and high energy, which suits workouts and intense moods.

## synthwave
Synthwave uses retro synthesizers and steady beats. It suits late-night coding, night drives and moody focus.
