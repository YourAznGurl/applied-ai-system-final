# Mood and Situation Guide

## Studying and focus
Situations: study, studying, homework, exam, exams, reading, concentrate, focus, library, test
Steady music with few lyrics helps concentration because it does not compete with thinking.
Recommended: genre=lofi; mood=focused; energy=0.35

## Workout and gym
Situations: workout, gym, lifting, running, exercise, pump, hype, high energy, training
Fast, loud, driving music raises heart rate and motivation during exercise.
Recommended: genre=rock; mood=intense; energy=0.9

## Heavy and aggressive
Situations: angry, rage, headbanging, heavy, aggressive, mosh, furious, vent
Loud, fast, distorted music gives anger somewhere to go.
Recommended: genre=metal; mood=aggressive; energy=0.95

## Party and dancing
Situations: party, dance, dancing, club, rave, festival, celebrate, celebration, euphoric
Big, bright, high-tempo electronic music gets a crowd moving.
Recommended: genre=edm; mood=euphoric; energy=0.9

## Road trip and driving
Situations: road trip, driving, car, highway, travel, trip, singalong
Upbeat, bright songs with a steady beat keep a long drive fun.
Recommended: genre=pop; mood=happy; energy=0.75

## Morning and waking up
Situations: morning, wake up, waking, sunrise, breakfast, fresh, walk
Gentle but bright songs lift your mood without being jarring.
Recommended: genre=indie pop; mood=happy; energy=0.6

## Coffee shop and relaxing
Situations: coffee, cafe, brunch, lounge, dinner, unwind, evening
Warm, mellow music makes a calm background for talking or relaxing.
Recommended: genre=jazz; mood=relaxed; energy=0.4

## Beach and summer
Situations: beach, summer, sunshine, vacation, island, barbecue, cookout
Laid-back rhythms match slow, sunny afternoons.
Recommended: genre=reggae; mood=relaxed; energy=0.5

## Rainy day and feeling sad
Situations: rainy, rain, sad, gloomy, melancholy, heartbroken, cloudy, lonely
Slow, quiet music lets you sit with the feeling instead of fighting it.
Recommended: genre=folk; mood=sad; energy=0.3

## Late-night coding
Situations: late night, midnight, coding, programming, deadline, night drive, developer
Steady electronic beats keep momentum going without distracting.
Recommended: genre=synthwave; mood=moody; energy=0.6

## Romantic date
Situations: date, romantic, love, anniversary, valentine, couple, slow dance
Smooth, warm music sets an intimate mood.
Recommended: genre=r&b; mood=romantic; energy=0.5

## Sleep and meditation
Situations: sleep, sleeping, bedtime, wind down, meditate, meditation, drifting, dreamy, space
Slow, spacious sounds help the mind slow down.
Recommended: genre=ambient; mood=chill; energy=0.2

## Peaceful and quiet
Situations: peaceful, quiet, gentle, soft, piano, orchestra, serene, tranquil
Soft instrumental music creates stillness.
Recommended: genre=classical; mood=peaceful; energy=0.2

## Nostalgia and throwbacks
Situations: nostalgic, nostalgia, throwback, memories, childhood, hometown, countryside, truck
Familiar, story-driven songs bring back old memories.
Recommended: genre=country; mood=nostalgic; energy=0.5

## Confidence and hype
Situations: confident, confidence, boss, interview, presentation, swagger, strut
Strong beats and bold delivery help you walk in feeling ready.
Recommended: genre=hip hop; mood=confident; energy=0.75
